# ShipPa

Ship public address. Every PA speaker anchored to a grid is part of that ship's network; there is nothing to wire.
Pilots use the PA panel on the shuttle console to set the situation code, sound general quarters, make announcements
and play internet sounds, and the collision warning sounds through it too. Speakers need power and distort when
damaged; a ship without speakers announces through its air alarms. Admins have `shippa_announce`, `shippa_code`,
`shippa_gq` and `shippa_speakers`.

Linked sounds queue. Pasting a link plays it at once on a quiet ship and adds it to the ship's queue otherwise; the next
link starts when a track ends or fails. The panel lists the playing track and the queue, with Skip, a remove button
per entry, Clear (the queue only) and Stop (the track and the queue). The queue belongs to the grid, holds
`ShipPaPlaylistComponent.MaxQueued` links and goes with the grid. Every link keeps the sound system's limits and the
console's request cooldown, and is checked when its turn comes: one that is refused is dropped, its requester is told,
and the queue moves on. The queue waits while no speaker works or the server is at its internet sound track cap.
Tracks run on the PA timeline whatever else is playing, so an alarm masks the music and the queue keeps its
schedule, as it does for a single track. There is a gap between tracks while the next one is fetched.

Entry points: `ShipPaSystem` (speakers, broadcasts, alarms), `ShipAlertSystem` (situation codes, general quarters,
announcements and the playlist messages from the console), `ShipPaPlaylistSystem` (the queue), `ShipAlarmPanel` (the
console panel), the client `ShipPaMeshSystem` (plays the ship's programme from real speaker positions), and
`ShipAlertCodePrototype` for the codes.
