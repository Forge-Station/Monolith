# The Common Writ of the Bench

*Draft for review.* The sector's in-character law. Under the Caelestinus Accord, signed by the TSF and the Vanguard
after the Collapse, the anchors and Caelestinus Central are held by the Bench: the Overseer and the judges, sworn to
neither navy. Judges ticket offenders from a Bench app on their PDA; the fine is taken from the offender's bank at once,
and anything they can't cover is taken from what they earn afterwards. In game, the Writ is a lore book page under
Caelestinus, and judges carry it as a book.

> By the hand of the Bench of Caelestinus. Binding on every soul within the anchors and beyond them. Ignorance of the
> Writ is no defence.

## Penalties

| Level | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Citation** | A fine. |
| **Impound** | A fine, and the vessel is held at Central until the Bench releases it. |
| **Banishment** | A fine, and the offender is barred from Caelestinus Central. |
| **Outlawry** | No fine. A warrant is issued with a bounty, and any citizen may bring the outlaw before the Bench. |

Fines show a default and, in brackets, the range a judge may set.

| Law | Text | Level | Fine |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Breach of the Anchors** | Raising a hand, weapon or tool against an anchor, its shield or its wardens, or cutting the power to a sanctioned relay. | Outlawry | – |
| **Trespass beyond the Anchors** | Entering Adrift or Untethered space without a Deep Writ. | Citation | 10,000 (5,000–20,000) |
| **Trespass in the Deep** | Entering the Deep without a Bench-sanctioned expedition. | Impound | 60,000 (50,000–100,000) |
| **Unsanctioned Landing** | Entering orbit around, or landing on, a world the Bench has not sanctioned. | Impound | 50,000 (30,000–80,000) |
| **Quarantine Breach** | Landing on a quarantine world, or carrying anything off one. | Banishment | 100,000 (75,000–150,000) |
| **Possession of Unlawful Material** | Holding unlawful material for personal use: a shard, a charm, a token. | Citation | 15,000 (10,000–25,000) |
| **Trafficking in Unlawful Material** | Mining, refining, selling, carrying in bulk or fitting unlawful material to a vessel. | Impound | 75,000 (50,000–120,000) |
| **Harbouring an Artifact** | Keeping, powering or wiring into a vessel any unregistered object recovered beyond the anchors. | Outlawry | – |
| **Sedition against the Anchors** | Preaching the unmooring of the sector, or belonging to any congregation that does. | Outlawry | – |

**Unlawful material** means:

- exotic matter, raw or refined, and anything built from it;
- any object recovered beyond the anchors that bears no registry mark, or that is warm, hums or gives power without a
  source;
- remains recovered beyond the anchors that were not returned through the Bench.

Surrendering unlawful material to the Bench is never a crime.

## General clauses

- **Drift is no defence.** A judge may take it into account in setting the penalty, but never in deciding guilt.
- **Declared war.** While war is declared, Murder, Assault and Piracy do not apply between combatants of the warring
  factions. This exception never covers neutrals or Vagrants, and never applies aboard Central.
- **Salvage right.** A vessel is derelict only when no holder of its deed is aboard or nearby and it has gone unclaimed
  for twenty minutes.
- **Custom charges.** The Bench may post ordinances for the round, or bring a charge not written here, with a fine and
  a level of its choosing.
- **Pardon.** A ticket may be pardoned only by the Bench. A pardon returns everything that was taken.

## How fines are collected

- The fine is taken from the offender's bank the moment the ticket is issued. The offender must be within a few steps
  of the judge.
- Whatever they can't cover becomes Bench debt for the round. Half of everything they earn goes to it until it is
  cleared.
- Debt still owed after 30 minutes, or over 50,000, marks them Wanted until it is paid.
- Fines have limits so a ticket can't be used to grief: a law counts once per ticket, a ticket carries at most 8
  charges, a custom charge or posted ordinance fines at most 100,000, one ticket fines at most 200,000, and one person
  is fined at most 400,000 in a round (pardoned tickets don't count). Beyond that the ticket still stands, but the
  fine stops.
- Outlawry tickets carry no fine: the offender is Wanted at once, with a bounty on the board.
