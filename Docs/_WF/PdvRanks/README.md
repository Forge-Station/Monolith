# PdvRanks

PDV chat ranks after the Ottoman army, picked in the loadout's chat rank tab and unlocked by playtime on the job,
the way the TSF ladder works. Each PDV job gets the ranks of its tier:

| Job | Ranks (hours on that job) |
|---|---|
| Rakhshan | Nefer, Onbaşı 10, Çavuş 30, Başçavuş 75 |
| Denasvar | Çavuş, Başçavuş 50, Mülazım-ı Sani 50, Mülazım-ı Evvel 100 |
| Asvaran | Mülazım-ı Evvel, Yüzbaşı, Kolağası 100 |
| Grand Vizier | Grand Vizier, Binbaşı, Kaymakam, Miralay |
| Jarrah (medic) | Sıhhiye Neferi, Sıhhiye Onbaşısı 10, Sıhhiye Çavuşu 30, Tabip Mülazım 75 |

Ranks show on radio messages in short form, like the TSF's `Pvt.`: Nfr., Onb., Çvş., BşÇvş., MlzS., MlzE., Yzb., Kol.,
Bnb., Kym., Mrl. and Vzr., and S.Nfr., S.Onb., S.Çvş. and Tbp.Mlz. for medics.

The Grand Vizier's fixed job rank moved into the ladder as its default. Infiltrators keep no rank so they can pass
unnoticed, and the pasha ranks (Mirliva and up) are left for admin and event roles. Ranks, gates and groups are in
`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/PdvRanks/ranks.yml`; the chat abbreviations are in `Resources/Locale/en-US/_WF/PdvRanks`.
