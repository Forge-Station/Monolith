# ArmorSpecialization

Worn-armour specializations that change what Wolfmed does to the body under a suit, so suits in a tier feel different
in a fight and not only on paper. Each is one component on the suit, and each respects the suit's armour coverage
(an item with no coverage list covers every part). The design and the faction assignments are in
`Docs/_WF/ArmorBalance/SPECIALIZATIONS.md`.

- `WFWoundWard`: refuses wounds outright (`blocks`) or makes a rule need a multiple of its usual damage (`hardens`).
  It answers Wolfmed's `WolfmedWoundRuleAttemptEvent`. The spall liner, sealed layer, insulation, tendon guards,
  concussion ward, bone plating, shock absorbers, trauma gel and cave-in rating are all this component with different
  wound lists, and `examine` names which one it is.
- `WFLimbLock`: covered limbs stay on through finishing hits and blasts, unless one damage type of the hit reaches
  `breakthrough` (zero means never). It answers `WolfmedSeverAttemptEvent`; surgery doesn't raise it.
- `WFPainDampener`: adds `relief` (15 by default; an analgesic gives 22) to the wearer's pain relief, through
  `WolfmedArmorPainReliefEvent`.
- `WFAblativeCoat`: a passive pool that soaks a share of one damage group (Burn by default) at the armour step, then
  recovers after a lull without hits.
- `WFAutoTourniquet` (server): ties a real Wolfmed tourniquet (`TourniquetSystem.Apply`) onto a covered limb with an
  untreated arterial bleed, spending a charge each time. Limbs only, so the necrosis clock and loosening work as normal;
  it never overrides a medic's treatment, works only while worn, and its charges come back over time.

`WFArmorSpecializationTest` covers each one through the real Wolfmed paths.
