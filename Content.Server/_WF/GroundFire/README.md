# GroundFire

Keeps a tile fire on planet ground (`ESTileFire` on a grid with `WFTerrainAtmosphereComponent`) to a small patch that
burns out. Upstream tile fires spread to every flammable tile with enough air and lived for good once they smouldered,
and a planet's air never runs out. A fire there may start at most `WFGroundFireSystem.MaxFires` fires between it and
everything it starts (the budget is split between parent and child on each spread), puts itself out after `Lifetime`
instead of smouldering on, and when it goes `AtmosphereSystem.WfRestoreBurntAir` takes the smoke, missing oxygen and
heat out of the tracked tiles around it. Fires on other grids are untouched. The hooks are in `ESTileFireSystem`.
