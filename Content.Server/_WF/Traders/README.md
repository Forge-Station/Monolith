# Traders

Dialogue NPCs that front real game systems: a vending machine's stock, shipyard console listings, a used ship lot,
generator refuelling and parcel packing. A customer talks to the trader, picks from the dialogue menu and pays; the
trader opens a shop, shipyard or used ships window, or answers in speech. A trader behind a table can be reached
across it.

Entry points: `TraderSystem` (dialogue, barter zone, payment and receipts), the services `TraderShopSystem`,
`TraderShipyardSystem`, `TraderUsedShipsSystem`, `TraderRefuelSystem` and `TraderParcelSystem`, `TraderComponent` and
`TraderDialoguePrototype`, and the client `TraderBoundUserInterface` with its windows. Traders are defined in
`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/Traders`. `IdCardOwnerSystem` (stamps an ID card with its owner at spawn) and
`InteractionRangeBonusEvent` (reach across the table) exist for traders only.

Payment is cash on the counter first, then the customer's bank for the rest, with the bank charged before any cash
is taken. The shipyard dealer's hosted console counts the counter as its cash slot. An Ironman's account is frozen,
so only the cash pays for them: a short counter is refused and nothing is taken.

Every living trader wears a question mark status icon in the SSD indicator's spot (`TraderIconSystem`, client).
Station traders are invulnerable and rooted. A killable trader (the wanderers, `killable: true`) keeps the body
that dies and runs: the wound system ignores damage totals, so it only kills through blood, breath and the organs,
and `MakeInert` leaves those alone for him. He has an HTN (`WFTraderCompound`) that never fights. `TraderFleeSystem`
notes whoever hurts him (`TraderFleeComponent`), ends any conversation, refuses to trade with them again and sends
him, through the HTN's `WFTraderFleeTarget` key, to the safe tile of his own ship farthest from them. He keeps away
while they stay near and calms down once they are far off or gone. He looks for that tile within 48 tiles of himself,
so a trader on a planet's ground grid doesn't search the whole world.

The planet roadside traders add three services. `TraderVehicleRepairSystem` (the mechanic, `RepairVehicle`) quotes the
nearest damaged vehicle parked within 16 m by its missing integrity times a share of its appraised value, and makes
the frame and every fitted part whole once paid. `TraderGoodsPaymentComponent` (the innkeeper) lets goods on the
counter, organs here, pay at half their appraisal for whatever cash leaves, with the rest back as change.
`TraderDevourSystem` (the innkeeper again) walks him, through `WFTraderMealTarget` in `WFTraderDevourCompound`, to anyone
dead or downed within 12 m of his post, eats them with his `Devourer`, and gives them all back up when he dies.
