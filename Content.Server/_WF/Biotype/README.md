# Biotype

Every species can be Organic or Synthetic. Organic is a flesh body: it breathes, eats, bleeds blood and is treated with
medicine. Synthetic is a machine like an IPC: a power cell, a positronic core and a coolant pump, hydraulic fluid, and
repair with welders and cable. Whichever a character is, Wolfmed runs that ladder for it (organic: brain, heart and
blood; machine: core, pump and power).

A species' native biotype is `biotype` on its `SpeciesPrototype`: Organic unless set, Synthetic for IPC and Synth. A
native mob is exactly the species' own prototype; a synthetic IPC is today's IPC. The character profile stores
`Biotype`; `Default` means native and validation (`EnsureValid`) turns it into the species' own, so a saved character
keeps its body. Players pick it with the Biotype switch (one icon box each, the icon is the prototype's `icon`) in the Body card of the character creator
(`Content.Client/_WF/Biotype/HumanoidProfileEditor.Biotype.cs`); it shows the species' own biotype until changed, and
switching species resets it to the new species' own.

A mob picked as the other biotype is converted live by `WFBiotypeSystem.Apply` with that biotype's `wfBiotype` overlay
(`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/Biotype/biotypes.yml`). `WFBiotypeSynthetic` gives an IPC body and the IPC's components;
`WFBiotypeOrganic` gives a human body and the organic species bases' components. Each strips the other side's
components (`remove`, `removeItemSlots`), adds `ensure` entries the mob lacks, replaces or adds `components`, refills
the bloodstream with the new fluid and rebuilds the body (`SharedBodySystem.WfRebuildBody`). Voice, emotes, languages,
inventory, sprite and markings are the species' own: a synthetic Vulpkanin looks and sounds like a Vulpkanin.
`WFBiotypeComponent` marks a mob whose profile was applied, and applying the same biotype again does nothing.

Two marked calls run it: `StationSpawningSystem.SpawnPlayerMob` before the loadout is equipped, and
`SharedHumanoidAppearanceSystem.LoadProfile` before `ProfileLoadFinishedEvent` (cloning, antag and ERT spawns, implants).
The default profile a humanoid loads as it spawns chose nothing and never converts it.

The body is rebuilt without the events that read as an injury: the old parts and organs are cut loose from the body,
`BodyComponent` is replaced and the old tree deleted, and the new component builds the new body on MapInit, as a fresh
spawn does. Hands are not touched, so items in hands stay held where the new body has the same hand (every body has
`left hand` and `right hand`); a hand the new body lacks drops its item at the mob's feet. Items in the old torso's
cavity slot are deleted with it. `WFBiotypeAppliedEvent` is raised on the mob and broadcast afterwards.

Known gaps: an organic IPC keeps the `ipc` inventory template (no ears slot, mask slot only for IPC masks), so it can't
wear a breath mask or headset; it keeps the radio trio for that reason. Genital organs already built on a mob are lost
when it is converted afterwards (a fresh spawn builds them after the conversion). A species' damage overlay groups the
new damage container lacks are dropped on the client (`DamageVisualsSystem`); an organic IPC shows no burn overlay.

Gates follow the effective biotype (`WFBiotypeGates.Effective`), not the species id, so a synthetic Vulpkanin is
treated as a machine. A trait lists `biotypeBlacklist` next to `speciesBlacklist`: the creator hides and drops it
(`TraitPrototype.Biotype.cs`), and `GetValidTraits` drops it on the server. `WFBiotypeLoadoutEffect` (`biotypes`) gates a
loadout; the oxygen and nitrogen breather groups require Organic, so only a flesh body is issued a survival box, and
IPC and Synth stay in the species lists so an organic one gets it. A synthetic body gets the Synthetics module's box instead. `WFBiotypeRequirement` (`biotypes`, `inverted`) gates
a role or antag; the zombie roles refuse Synthetic. Species identity (the IPC name rule, genitals, inventory template,
markings) stays keyed on the species.
