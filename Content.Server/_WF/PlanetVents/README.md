# PlanetVents

Carcinoma breathes. Its air is mostly carbon dioxide (3 kPa oxygen, 12 kPa nitrogen, 90 kPa carbon dioxide at 37 °C),
so nobody walks it without internals, and ventricles, fleshy mounds round a ring of openings, breathe it in and out.
They grow in wide fields over part of the surface, a few to a screen there, and along the caverns' throats; the biome
never lays them on a road, trail or site, which are pinned before the ground loads.

Each vent (`WFPlanetVentComponent`, run by `WFPlanetVentSystem`) rests 6 to 14 seconds, then inhales for four
(a wet heave, the mound sinks, its tile loses 2 mol of air) and exhales: a warm puff rises (`WFCarcinomaVentPuff`) and
6 mol of carbon dioxide at 45 °C go into its tile. Open ground shares the world's fixed air, so only a tile atmos
tracks, such as a room walled round a vent, fills up. A vent only breathes with a player within 24 metres, and only
exists where the ground is loaded. It bleeds and leaves meat, sometimes a pair of lungs, when destroyed.

The world's own life doesn't breathe its air: `WFPlanetNativesSystem` (Planets module) gives `BreathingImmunity` to
whatever Carcinoma's wildlife tables spawn and its slime breeds, while they are on it.

Art: tgstation's lavaland tumor and steam clouds, rebuilt by `Tools/_WF/PlanetVents/build_vent_sprites.py`. Sounds:
Black Mesa gonarch breaths and a gas hiss, imported by `Tools/_WF/PlanetVents/import_vent_sounds.py`.
