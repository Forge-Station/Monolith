# PlanetCargoHubs

Some rounds a cargo depot is built on a planet instead of in space. Each of depots A and B has a
`wf.planet_cargo_hub_chance` (default 0.35) to go down, and if neither rolls, one is picked anyway: at least one depot
is on a planet every round that has a world to take it (a chance of 0 turns hubs off). The first world to place its sites in a round rolls for
both and picks each a world among those built so far: one its `wfPlanetCargoHub` prototype lists, with roads and no
fixed city on it (the city's station already holds that ground), and no other hub. With caverns, cities and prebuild
as they are, that is Merak, Fervidus, Thrascias or Aerumna (Asclepiu too when fixed cities are off; never Carcinoma).
The hub is added to that world's sites through `WFPlanetPoiExtraSitesEvent` and placed before the table's own, on the
road network. Once the world is preloaded, `WFPlanetCargoHubSystem` makes the depot's station over the ground from the
space depot's game map, named "Cargo Depot A" or "B" with the same `TradeCrateDestination` letter, so trade crates
bought anywhere for that letter sell at the hub's pallets. Its orbit signal is identified from the start, so it shows
by name in the planet signals list, on the planet map and in the planet scan. When the space depots spawn at round
start, `PointOfInterestSystem.GenerateDepots` asks `IsPlanetside` for each letter and skips any that already stands on
a planet; a hub that found no room leaves its depot in space as usual. Nothing is planned after the space depots spawn.

The hubs (`Resources/Maps/_WF/PlanetCargoHubs`, written by `python Tools/_WF/PlanetCargoHubs/hubs.py`) face south onto
the road: three berths (`cargo_hub_triple`, depot A) or two (`cargo_hub_twin`, depot B), each a 24 by 36 asphalt pad
edged in warning lines with floodlights round it and a numbered landing-zone sign at its head, then a loading apron
with six sell pallets and a high-market pallet console per berth, and a roofed warehouse and office across the north
with the depot's turn-in chute and trade goods vendor, powered by an RTG. There is no conveyor or loading arm: crates
are carried or dragged from a landed ship to the pallets at the head of its berth. Ships set down on the berths with
the planet flight rules; landed hulls on a planet are left alone by grid cleanup. `WFCargoHubBerth` marks each pad's
south-west tile and size.

A planetside depot is made hard to miss (`WFPlanetCargoHubSystem.Notice`): one global announcement a round, at round
start or as soon as the hub is built if the round is already under way ("Cargo Depot A has set down on Merak; land on
its berths."), and the same line when a trade crate bound for it is examined. An FTL beacon (`WFCargoHubFtlBeacon`,
"Cargo Depot A (Merak)") sits on the sector map just inside the world's disc, so the destination list and the shuttle
map list it like the space depots. Its site is a `Depot` on the planet map (`WFPlanetCargoHubSites` tells a hub's site
by name): a ringed crate glyph in the space depots' IFF green, named at any zoom and shown from orbit; the planet scan
callout names it under the world's title, and the world's radar tag out in space names it too. On the ground a tall
mast with a slowly pulsing green light (`WFCargoHubBeacon`) stands between the first two berths and amber strobes
(`WFCargoHubBerthStrobe`) blink at each berth's mouth. The landing guide only reads the tiles under the hull, so it
does not mark the berths.
