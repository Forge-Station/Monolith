# WildSlimes

Each world can have a slime breed of its own that no other breed mutates into, so players fly there to catch it and
bring it home to breed. Carcinoma's is the carcinoma slime, which breeds true and now and then into the metastatic
slime, a breed that only comes from breeding it. Both shrug off the Gut's digestive acid, heal while standing in blood
and still hate water.

The slimes live in small patches of see-through flesh jelly on the surface, each over a slime grotto in the cavern below
that shows through the jelly, with a one-tile shaft from the patch's middle down into it. While a player is near
(`WFDormancySystem.IsActiveAt`), `WFWildSlimeSystem` keeps each patch and grotto stocked up to its cap; once nobody has
been near for 30 s the slimes are retired, and they come back when someone returns. A slime that is caught, tamed,
killed or carried off its world leaves a place that refills after the respawn delay.

A wild slime (`WFWildSlimeComponent`) is in the Passive faction and runs `WFWildSlimeCompound`: it latches only onto
whoever hurts it (`NPCRetaliation`), walks back when it strays past its patch (`WFWildSlimeHomeOperator`) and otherwise
idles. Its hunger and thirst are held steady, so it never starves, grows or splits. Sucked into a xenovac, petted
(tamed) or taken to another map than its patch's ground or grotto, it becomes an ordinary xenobiology slime.

## Adding a world's slime

All YAML, as `Resources/Prototypes/_WF/WildSlimes/carcinoma.yml` does:

1. Two `breed`s in `breeds.yml` that only list each other in `potentialMutations`, their extracts in `extracts.yml`.
2. A patch tile (`transparent: true`, an id the planet map colours right) and a `biomeTemplate` laying it, reached
   from a top-level `BiomeMetaLayer` of the world's surface biome (Carcinoma nests the patch noise in a sparse zone
   noise so a world has only a handful). For a grotto, a cavern tile and the same nesting added last to the world's
   cavern biome with the same noises: each seed is the surface one minus the cavern's `seedOffset`, and slightly lower
   thresholds make the grotto wider than the patch.
3. A `wfWildSlimeHabitat` naming the surface, breed, patch template and tiles, and the caps and respawn delay.

Entry points: `WFWildSlimeSystem` (patches found on `WFPlanetNetworkBuiltEvent`, `Populate`, `MakeWild`, `Release`),
`WFReagentBathHealSystem`, and `XenobiologySystem.SpawnSlimeAt`. Art: `Tools/_WF/WildSlimes/gen_jelly_art.py`.

To put patches beside a feature, use the feature's own noise as the zone and lay the patch tile with that noise turned
over (`invert: true`, threshold the feature's negated), so the patch stops where the feature starts; Merak's glass
rings the oases and Thrascias's rime the crystal fields this way.

## Other worlds

Each in its own `<world>.yml`, `.ftl`, texture folder and `<World>SlimesTest.cs`, with art from
`Tools/_WF/WildSlimes/gen_world_jelly_art.py` and the shared checks in `WildSlimeWorldChecks`.

- Verda (Asclepiu): the dew slime and the bloom slime. Water heals them; they breed only at 283-303 K sitting in water.
  Pond jelly ringed by reeds over flooded grottos.
- Merak: the sandglass slime and the amber slime. Slow, water heals them, they spill water when they die and dig in
  when hurt (`WFDigInComponent`, `WFSpillOnDeathComponent`); they breed only at 305-340 K. Fused glass at the oases'
  edges over fossil-bed grottos.
- Thrascias: the rime slime and the aurora slime. They breathe nothing, keep a 190 K body that cold can't hurt and warm
  air does, and plasma puddles heal them; they breed only at 150-240 K. Rime ice by the crystal fields over ice
  grottos with a plasma lake.
- Fervidus: the cinder slime and the obsidian slime. They glow ember orange, need no air and shrug off heat and
  fire, but water steams them badly. Obsidian glass over glass vaults round a magma pool.
- Aerumna: the umbral slime and the lumen slime. Near-black and half unseen, needing next to no air; the lumen
  glows. Pink crystal in the fungal forest over geode chambers; the xenonids leave wild ones alone (PlanetNatives).

The Fervidus obsidian glass has eight variants, an organic pattern with no repeated shape, which `Tools/_WF/WildSlimes/gen_slime_tiles.py` paints (and a contact sheet with its argument); the art is generated, not copied. The other worlds' ground tiles come from `gen_jelly_art.py` and `gen_world_jelly_art.py`.
