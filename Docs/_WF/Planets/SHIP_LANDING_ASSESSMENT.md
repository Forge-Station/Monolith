# Stock vessel landing assessment: Atmosphere Mode

Updated 2026-09-18. Actual hull fixture mass and installed thrust were measured by loading all 144 purchasable vessel definitions. This report applies the new atmospheric efficiency to those measurements.

## Rules

- Ordinary linear engines: 50% thrust, 3x normal electrical demand below orbit.
- Converted landing engines: full rated thrust and normal electrical demand.
- Lift ratio = sum of working engines' atmospheric force / (grid fixture mass x 9.81 x planet gravity).
- A ratio of 1 is the hover boundary. Extra lift provides manoeuvring and climbing margin; aim above 1.2.
- Power loss removes lift immediately; a recovered engine must remain powered for two seconds before contributing lift again.

These lists assume all engines are ordinary and functional. They are thrust limits, not guarantees of electrical endurance: electrical brownouts can disqualify otherwise capable ships. Faction, special and carrier-only catalogs are included. Actual docked sets pool thrust and mass; damaged or modified ships differ. Game grid fixture mass is not realistic cargo displacement.
