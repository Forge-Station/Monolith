# NpcIntents

How an NPC's fight with a player ends. A `wfNpcIntentSet` (`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/NpcIntents/sets.yml`) is a
faction's weighted list of endpoints; an NPC with a `WFNpcIntent` component rolls one when it picks a target, and
anything without the component keeps fighting to the death (monsters, xenos, cavern horrors, hostile ships' crews).

- **HoldUp**: aims, says a sum (Fluent), and waits `demandSeconds`. The player pays by handing a stack of thalers or
  something worth the sum to the NPC, dropping the cash beside it, or with the "Pay" verb on the NPC (bank). Paid, the
  gang walks off; refusing, running past `runRange`, drawing a gun, hitting it or letting the time run out starts the
  fight, as the set's `refusal` endpoint. Needs a brain with the hold branch (`WFIntentHold`, see
  `WFCavernRaiderCompound`), so set `holds: true`; on any other brain it is never rolled.
- **Mug**: fights until the target is down (not alive, or not `Up` in Wolfmed terms), then stops, walks to them, takes
  their thalers and one valuable (their weapon some of the time), and leaves.
- **BeatDown**: fights until the target is down, says its line and leaves.
- **Harvest**: fights until the target is down, then takes an organ (`harvestOrgans`) or a limb (`harvestLimbChance`,
  `harvestLimbs`) with the Wolfmed body APIs and leaves. The victim lives and needs a medic. Reavers and cultists.
- **WarnOff**: says a warning, fires past the target, gives `warnSeconds`, then chases them out and stops when they are
  `warnRange` away or down.
- **Lethal**: no change; the NPC never spares anyone.

A non-lethal NPC puts a downed target on its ignore list (`NpcFactionSystem.UnignoreEntity` takes them off again),
drops its combat components, and cancels any melee or gun attempt at a downed target. After its endpoint it leaves
that player alone for `cooldown` (600 s), unless they hit it. A gang of the same set within 12 tiles takes up the same
endpoint together; one speaks and takes.

`boarder: true` (Reavers): a crewman aboard a ship that is not its post's fires on nobody who has not hit its party
(`HoldsFireOn`, checked by `WFCrewWeaponSystem.CanEngage`) for 90 s after the blow, so boarding parties loot and fight
only when hit.

Sets: `WFIntentsTunnelRaider` and `WFIntentsBandit` (tunnel raiders, gunners), `WFIntentsReaver` (the pirate crew
profile), `WFIntentsCultist` (blood cultists), `WFIntentsMugger` (strangers' muggers) and `WFIntentsSettler` (no NPC uses it yet).
