# Wolfmed

Wolfgate's medical overhaul. It ports the Space Onyx wound system (Onyx `2f5bab9`, kept under its `_Onyx`
folders) onto Shitmed's body graph and builds Wolfgate's own model of dying on top of it: consciousness, pain
and shock, bleeding and blood volume, organ damage, fractures, burns, infection, embedded objects, amputation and
reattachment, temperature, toxins and radiation, and death as a brain that stops rather than a damage total. A
wound host (`WoundHostComponent`: every organic species and the IPC chassis) takes damage as wounds on its parts,
and the mob-state thresholds, the health alert, the damage overlay, health bars, examine and the health analyzer
all read those wounds instead of the flat damage total.

What players use: the health analyzer's wound readout, vitals card and treatment advice; the field medicine in
the Wolfgate vendor (splints, antiseptic, skin grafts, analgesic, stimulant and opiate pens, hydraulic fluid for a
chassis); the IV drip (`WolfmedIvDripSystem`: blood packs or a beaker fed in, or blood drawn out, at a set flow);
Shitmed's surgeries with Wolfmed's steps (tend wounds, weld chassis, rewire); the autodoc pod (queued
and AUTO-triaged procedures, a voice, a sealed atmosphere, welder repair, a blood reservoir); the synthetic HUD and health bars for
IPCs; the crawl, call-for-help and dying actions of a downed body. Admins have the `damage` command's body-part
form (`DamageCommand.Wolfmed.cs`) and the debug crate.

Entry points: `WolfmedDamageableSystem` (`Content.Shared/_WF/Wolfmed/Compat`, the seam every damage change
passes through), `WoundDamageRoutingSystem` (`Content.Shared/_Onyx/Wounds`), `SharedWolfmedConsciousnessSystem`
and `WolfmedLifeSystem` (the clock from wounded to dead), `AutodocSystem` (`Content.Server/_WF/Wolfmed/Autodoc`)
and the analyzer partials in `Content.Server/_WF/Wolfmed/Medical`.

Design record, in `Docs/_WF/Wolfmed`: `DECISIONS.md` (every decision by milestone and playtest; append to it),
the death plan (`WOLFMED_DEATH_PLAN.md`) and `WOLFMED_MANIFEST.md`, the port-time file manifest. The generated list below is the list of record for the
files the module edits outside `_WF`.

Tests: `Content.IntegrationTests/Tests/_WF/Wolfmed` and `Tests/_Onyx/Wounds`. Run the filter
`FullyQualifiedName~_Onyx.Wounds|FullyQualifiedName~_Onyx.Body|FullyQualifiedName~Wolfmed|FullyQualifiedName~GibTest|FullyQualifiedName~Tests.Body|FullyQualifiedName~Autodoc`
and rerun any Skipped test alone: the fixture reports an assertion that fails inside a pair callback as
Skipped ("dirty-disposed").
