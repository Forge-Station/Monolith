# PlanetMap

A conventional map of a world on the radar, in place of the old sampled tile colours. On or over a planet, the
shuttle console, radar consoles and a vehicle's mass scanner draw the world as a map under the radar contacts: flat
ground in the world's base colour with smoothed patches of water, lava, sand, rock and the like, the roads to scale
with their shoulders, footpaths dashed, tunnels darker and dashed, the world's edge, a dashed ring round the landing
zone and a glyph for every site. Unknown sites show a "?"; once someone on the ground identifies one it shows its
kind (house, fuel stop, vault, facility, camp, wreck, building) and, zoomed in, its name. A city's outline shows from
orbit. The radar's Map toggle (top right) turns it off.

`WFPlanetMapSystem` (server) keeps a networked `WFPlanetMapComponent` on each world's orbit layer, which every client
has. Once the network is built it samples the terrain recipe once every eight or more tiles (never loading ground),
run-length encoded and at most 129 by 129 cells. Once the ground's points of interest are placed
(`WFPlanetPoiComponent.NetworkReady`, and on `WFPlanetPreloadedEvent`) it thins the road centreline, footpaths and
tunnels to polylines (`WFPlanetMapGeometry`: Zhang-Suen thinning, junction-to-junction chains, Ramer-Douglas-Peucker),
at most 4000 points a world. Every second it checks the sites' signals and resends only the site list when one is
identified. The client (`ShuttleNavControl.PlanetMap`, `WFPlanetMapMesh`) builds the ground mesh by marching squares
when the terrain changes and the line meshes when the zoom changes, and culls both by box.
