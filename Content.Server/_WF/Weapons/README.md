# Weapons

Client-predicted guns and some weapon content. The shooter's client fires its own copy of each projectile and hitscan
beam, so shots appear the moment they're fired; the server links its copy to the prediction and hides it from the
shooter. When the server fires fewer rounds than a request predicted (a burst it carried on itself, a late request it
had already caught up), it names the unfired copies in `WFPredictedShotsRefusedEvent` and the shooter's client drops
them, so no phantom round flies on. Also material bullet impact and punch sounds ported from Skyrat-tg, and a Mosin loadout for the Contractor.

Entry points: the `GunSystem.Prediction` partials (shared, client and server), `impacts.yml` with the sounds under
`Resources/Audio/_WF/Weapons`, and `WFContractorMosinLoadout`. `WFGunPredictionTest` covers prediction.

Upstream's shield rule lets only ballistic sidearms fire alongside a shield. A gun is exempt through
`CanShootWithShield`; `enabled: false` takes the exemption back off a gun that inherits it, such as the HCLM.
`ShieldSidearmTest` covers the HCLM and the Anaconda family.

Impact blasts stay on the hull they hit. The projectile sweep holds each hit on the struck grid, and `QueueExplosion`
keeps a grid-held entity in that grid's frame even off its tiles. Before, a shell detonating on a hull's outer face was
queued in map coordinates and spawned a tick later, so a ship closing faster than a wall's width per tick carried the
blast inside an intact wall. `WFHullImpactTest` covers flat and diagonal walls at closing speeds up to 40 m/s.

A ship gun with `WFLoaderAutoFeed` refills from its linked ammo loaders after each shot, so a single-round gun such as
the CHARON keeps firing without a click in the loader window per slug. `WFLoaderAutoFeedTest` covers the CHARON.

Guns above 5 shots/s play the networked gunshot sound at most once per 0.2 s. The shooter still hears every shot
predicted on their own client; this only cuts the audio entities other players receive (`GunSystem.WFGunshotAudio.cs`,
`GunshotAudioThrottleTest`).
