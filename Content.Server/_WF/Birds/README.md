# Birds

Planet birds that perch, peck about, take off when someone comes close and land somewhere else. Each world has its own
species: Verda songbirds and lake gulls on Asclepiu, glass-winged swifts on Merak, snow grouse on Thrascias, ash hawks
on Fervidus, carrion birds on Aerumna and flesh kites on Carcinoma. Ash hawks dive at a player walking alone; carrion
birds circle anything carrying 30 or more damage and come down to peck at it. All of them can be shot or hit, and
butcher into meat.

- Spawning: each world's fauna tables (`WFFauna<Planet><Region>`, `_WF/Planets`) roll a bird like any other animal, so
  `WFPlanetFaunaSystem` caps and retires it. That bird leads a flock: on map init it spawns `minFlock`-`maxFlock` minus
  one copies of itself round it. Those members retire themselves once no player has been within 96 m for two minutes.
- Behaviour (`WFBirdSystem`): no HTN and no pathfinding. A bird glides straight at its goal and decides four times a
  second. On the ground it hops about until its perch time runs out, something alive comes within `nearRange`, or
  something moving faster than `scareSpeed` comes within `scareRange`; then the whole flock takes off, away from the
  startler. In the air its fixtures collide with nothing but bullets, so it crosses walls; it may circle the landing
  spot for a while, then lands on the nearest clear, unroofed tile. Members follow the leader and land round it.
- Hostile birds (`WFBirdHostile`): spot prey within `diveRange`, optionally circle it for `circleTime`, dive at
  `diveSpeed`, strike once with their `MeleeWeapon`, climb away and come round again after `cooldown`. Hurting a hostile
  bird makes the attacker its prey; hurting any other bird puts its flock up.
- Visuals (`WFBirdVisualizerSystem`): the `Body` layer switches between `sit`, `fly` and `dead`, lifted by
  `flightLift` while flying, with the `Shadow` layer (a silhouette of the flight frames) left on the ground and the
  sprite drawn above walls.
- Sprites: recoloured tgstation parrot and pAI hawk/crow sprites, built by `Tools/_WF/Birds/build_bird_sprites.py`.
  Sounds: the owner's Black Mesa boid, bird and hawk calls, imported by `Tools/_WF/LivingWorld/import_bms.py --birds`
  into `Resources/Audio/_WF/Birds` (`BirdTest` fails on an unused file or more than 20); the take-off flap is Mono's
  CC0 hydrakin flap.
