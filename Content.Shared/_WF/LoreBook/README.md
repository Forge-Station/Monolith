# LoreBook

A book-themed window that holds the setting's lore, split out of the guidebook. Any guide entry with `lore: true`, and
every child under it, belongs to the lore book: the guidebook leaves it out, and any request for it (a job's help, a
book's `GuideHelp`, a `textlink`) opens the lore book at that page instead. Pages are numbered in reading order, with a
searchable contents page on the left.

Players open it from the book button on the top bar, the game menu or the lobby. Guide pages link into it with
`[lorelink="text" link="EntryId"]`, a gold link with a book icon. Entities with a lore page get a Lore button on
examine: list them in a `wfLoreLink` prototype (children of a listed prototype inherit it), or give them a
`GuideHelp` that points at a lore page.

Some pages unsettle the book. A guide entry's `unease` (0 to 1) builds up as it is read in an open book
(`WFLoreBookWindow.Unease.cs`): letters on every page start to slip, then the book writes lines of its own between
paragraphs (`wf-lore-book-whisper-*`), and deep in, its title, chapter trail and page numbers slip too. Closing the
book and opening it again clears it. A page stays as it was drawn until it is turned again; now and then a word is written in the DS13 glyph font
(`WFLoreGlyphs`), and quiet whispers play under the book (`WFLoreBookWhispers`), more often and louder as it corrupts.
The Lattice's pages (the Unmooring, the Lattice, the Choir) carry unease. Pages turn with one of two page-flip sounds (`WFLoreBookPageTurn`).

Raising `WFLoreBookUIController.Edition` opens the book once for every player, at its front, on their next lobby
or round; `wf.lorebook.seen_edition` (client, saved) remembers who has seen it.

Entry points: `WFLoreBookUIController` (open, toggle, link redirects), `WFLoreBookWindow`, `WFLoreBookSystem` (which
entries and entities are lore, the examine verb), `WFLoreLinkTag`, `GuideEntry.Lore` and `WFLoreLinkPrototype`.
Textures come from `Tools/_WF/LoreBook/make_textures.py`. Lore pages live in `Resources/ServerInfo/_WF/LoreBook` and
in the upstream `_Mono` lore, factions and star system pages.
