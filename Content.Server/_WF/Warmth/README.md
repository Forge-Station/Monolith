# Warmth

Tells players how warm they are and gives fires, heaters, coats and shelter a job. Each second `WFWarmthSystem` reads
every awake body: the air temperature on its own tile, how its body feels (Freezing, Cold, Chilly, Comfortable, Warm,
Hot, Overheating, from Wolfmed's core temperature against its hypothermia and heat exhaustion lines), how wet it is, and
the wind on an open tile. The planet timepiece panel shows "Here" and "Outside" temperatures and the feel word; off a
planet a small readout appears while the air or the body is worth a look (vacuum, under 10 °C or over 35 °C, or any feel
but Comfortable, or a fire reaches the body).

Both panels carry a temperature gauge: a bar from freezing to hot (the cold harm line to the heat harm line, from the
same shares the feel word reads) with a marker where the body sits, an arrow for whether it is warming or cooling, and
a "Near warmth" line while a lit fire or radiant heater reaches it. `WFWarmthSystem.UpdateGauge` sets it for players'
bodies only.

Halfway from normal to the line where Wolfmed harms the body (`wf.warmth.warning_share`), the warning signs start:
shivering or sweating messages, a visible shiver emote or sweat popup, a 10% (cold) or 5% (heat) slowdown and a frost
or heat-shimmer screen effect that grows toward the harm line. The skin nearing its own damage threshold warns too.

Rain and snow soak a body standing in the open (heavy rain in a minute, a blizzard in two); it dries over ten minutes
in the open, faster under cover and much faster by a fire. Wet clothes lose up to 50% more heat, and wind on an open
tile up to twice as much (`wf.warmth.wet_cooling`, `wf.warmth.wind_chill_per_speed`, `wf.warmth.wind_chill_max`).
Lit fires warm bodies directly through Mono's `EntityRadiusHeater`, now on bonfires, fireplaces, burning barrels,
braziers (while burning) and running space heaters, with a limit at body temperature so a fire never burns.
Clothing with `TemperatureProtection` says on examine how cold it keeps you warm to on its own.

Measured (WarmthTest, unprotected human, -16 °C blizzard): chilly at once, warnings at 250 s, Downed at 565 s, so
about five minutes of warning and no cold damage before it. In a winter coat, or by a bonfire, the body stays
comfortable; a body brought to the fire at the warning line is comfortable again within five seconds.

Entry points: `WFWarmthSystem.Tick`, `WFWarmthComponent`, `EntityRadiusHeaterSystem.IsActive`, the client
`WFWarmthSystem` (compact readout, overlay) and `WFPlanetTimepieceHudSystem` (planet panel).
