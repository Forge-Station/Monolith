# Species

Playable species ported from HardLight and Starlight, plus Wolfgate's own Canine. The module holds what they need
beyond the upstream species pipeline: markings (including scars and tattoos any species can use), voices and speech
emotes, languages, metabolizer types, the Skrell damage modifier set, typing indicators, silicon screams, meat, the
Rodentia squeeze under tables, Avali chemistry (amoxla, its auto-injector and the reagent rules that make saline,
dexalin and iron poison Avali while ammonia heals their airloss), and guidebook pages. Its marked edits add the
species to upstream marking, clothing and species prototypes, let Reptilians wear hair and facial hair, and make
explicit clothing layers pick their species states so Avali wear Avali hardsuits. The creator UI is in Humanoid.

Vulpkanin and Canine wear Starlight's Vulpkanin body (`Resources/Textures/_WF/Species/Mobs/Species/Vulpkanin`, written
by `Tools/_WF/LegStyle/port.py`; Canine keeps its own head) and start with Starlight's fox tail and ears. They stand
digitigrade by default; the LegStyle module holds both pairs of legs and the plantigrade option.

Eye markings have their own `Eyes` category, so left and right eyes no longer compete with face markings for the
head's points; no species limits it.

## Mismatched parts

A per-character checkbox in the creator's Body card, saved with the profile, as on Nova Sector. With it on, the
character may wear every species' markings, hair and facial hair, and the skin takes any colour even where the species
limits it to skin tones (`MismatchedPartsRules.SkinColoration`, used by validation, `LoadProfile` and the creator's
skin picker). The species' point budgets still apply, and a
category the species has no points for gets one. Body parts only some species' sprites have (Resomi tail extras,
harpy arm extensions) stay with those species. Random profiles leave it off, and turning it off in the creator drops
whatever only the option allowed.

`MismatchedPartsRules` holds the rules. `MarkingSet.ForProfile` and `OpenPoints` open the budgets, and the
`EnsureSpecies` overload keeps other species' markings; validation, the body (`LoadProfile`), the creator doll and the
creator's marking picker all go through them. Hair and facial hair are saved outside the marking set, so a style the
species can't wear on its own is added as a forced marking after the normal hair path.
`HumanoidAppearanceComponent.MismatchedParts` lets the client draw and colour markings without a species layer.

A species wears a hair category on its own when its marking points allow it, its sprite set has a layer for it that
takes markings, and it has at least one style; without the option, a species that can't is offered none. The barber
mirror doesn't know about the option yet, so it only offers the species' own hair.

## Chassis

An IPC's body parts are markings. They have their own `Chassis` category, so Head, Chest, Arms and Legs points are
left for ordinary markings; before, a wing or scar filed under Chest swapped the torso out. A chassis holds one part
per body part: `MarkingSet.EnsureChassis` (run from `EnsureDefault`) drops extras and puts the species' default on an
empty part, and the creator's picker swaps a part in place and puts the default back when one is taken off. The
Bishop and Hephaestus limbs other species can wear are chassis parts too, and cost them no points.

## Tests

`ShadekinNightVisionTest` covers Shadekin night vision, `AvaliChemistryTest` and `AvaliClothingTest` the Avali
reagent rules and hardsuits, and `EyeMarkingsTest` the eye markings beside a full head. `MismatchedPartsTest` covers
the option's rules, validation, the saved column, random profiles, and the hair, facial hair and another species'
marking on the bodies and creator dolls of every playable species; `MismatchedPartsBodyTest` a networked body and its
clone. `ChassisMarkingsTest` covers the chassis rules on a body and draws every marking an IPC may pick on its doll
and its own sprite.
