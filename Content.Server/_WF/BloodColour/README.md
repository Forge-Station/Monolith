# BloodColour

Players can give their character a blood and flesh colour of their own in place of the species'. The Body card in the
character creator (`Content.Client/_WF/BloodColour/BloodColourPicker.cs`, under the leg toggle) has a "Custom blood
colour" toggle and a colour picker; off keeps the species' own. The profile stores it as `BloodColor` (null for the species' own), saved in
the `blood_color` profile column as hex.

On profile load `WFBloodColourSystem` puts a `WFBloodColourComponent` on the mob and repaints its bloodstream. The
colour rides on the blood itself as `WFBloodColourData` reagent data, added by `BloodstreamSystem.GetEntityBloodData`,
and `Solution.GetColor` prefers it over the reagent's colour. Puddles, footprints, blood bags, IV lines and anything
else coloured by its solution follow it, and blood taken from the character keeps the colour. The reagent stays the
species' own, so chemistry, transfusions and Wolfmed fluid matching are unchanged.

Wolfmed wound, gib and gore overlays use `WolfmedGoreSystem.GetBloodColor`, which returns the chosen colour. On the
client `WFBloodColourVisualsSystem` tints the brute damage overlays. A mob whose blood reagent changes after the colour
was set (zombification) bleeds the new reagent's colour; its brute overlays keep the chosen tint.
A part cut off a coloured body gets the component too, so a severed limb's wound art keeps the colour.
