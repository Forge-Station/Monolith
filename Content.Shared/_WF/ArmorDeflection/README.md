# ArmorDeflection

Rule-of-cool plating for high-end armour: light rounds ricochet off it. `WFArmorDeflectComponent` on a worn suit puts
`WFArmorDeflectWearerComponent` on its wearer, which answers `ProjectileReflectAttemptEvent` before the round deals
any damage. A round that qualifies turns back the way it came within a 120° spread and becomes the wearer's, so it can
hit anyone but them, the original shooter included.

A round qualifies when its Piercing is at or under the threshold (24 by default), after soft rounds (negative armour
penetration, such as hollow points and RIP) are scaled down by it. Rounds at 0.3 armour penetration or more, and rounds
that ignore resistances, always get through. At 24, 9mm, .45, 5.7×28 and 5.56 FMJ bounce; 7.62×39 FMJ, 7.62×51,
.357, .50 BMG and every AP round get through. Hitscan, melee and lasers are unaffected.

The PDV Juggernaut and the Ullman VK-1 Director use it. `WFArmorDeflectTest` checks the threshold against the real
ammunition prototypes.
