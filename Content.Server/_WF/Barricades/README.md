# Barricades

RMC-14's directional barricades (MIT), with cmss13 sprites and sounds (CC-BY-SA-3.0). A barricade sits on one edge
of its tile and faces the way it was built; one barricade per facing per tile. They are climbable, take barbed wire
(hurts melee attackers, blocks climbing, +50 health) and are welded to repair. Melee attacks from in front of an
anchored barricade can hit the barricade instead of whoever is behind it, more often the healthier it is.

- Built from the construction menu: metal, plasteel, wooden, folding metal and folding plasteel barricades,
  turnstile, handrail, plastic road barrier, barbed wire. Taken apart by unanchoring (wrench), screwdriver, crowbar.
- Metal and plasteel barricades take composite, biohazard or reinforced upgrades: use two steel sheets on one for a
  radial menu; a multitool removes the upgrade and refunds the steel.
- Folding barricades open and close by click. A crowbar links folding plasteel barricades so a row moves together.
- MB-6 folding barricade (`CMFoldingBarricade`): use in hand to deploy where you stand, facing your way; drag it onto
  yourself, use the verb or a crowbar to fold it back. Each folded MB-6 keeps its damage; weld the stack to repair.
- Sandbags: an unfolded entrenching tool digs natural ground (dirt, sand, grass, snow) to fill empty sandbags next
  to you; five full sandbags build a sandbag barricade. The tool takes sandbag barricades back apart.
- Spawn-only: snow, HESCO and stacked barricades, the medical turnstile and the map handrail variants.

Entry points: `BarricadeSystem` (placement, sandbags, entrenching tool), `SharedBarbedSystem`,
`BarricadeMeleeBlockSystem` (hooked from `SharedMeleeWeaponSystem.AttemptAttack`), `RMCFoldingBarricadeSystem`,
`RMCFoldingBarricadeLinkingSystem`, `RMCUpgradeSystem`, `BarricadeTurnstileSystem`.

## Differences from RMC

- Xeno, skill, acid, nailgun and RMC construction-menu hooks are dropped; anyone can do everything.
- Repairs use the upstream `Repairable` (welder, full repair, 10 s) instead of RMC's partial weld.
- Materials are Wolfgate's steel, plasteel, wood and plastic; upgrades take any steel stack instead of an upgrade item.
- The barbed wire overlay is drawn (RMC never mapped the layer). Stacked barricades can be destroyed.
- Barricades block mobs like tables, not bullets (RMC leaves bullets as a TODO too).
- The turnstile is a cut-down copy of upstream SS14's, as `BarricadeTurnstile`, with a sensor fixture.
