# Boombox

A portable speaker (`WFBoombox`), a carried version of the ship PA's internet sounds. Use it to open its window:
a display with the playing track and progress, Skip, Stop, Shuffle, Repeat, a volume knob, a slot for links and the
queue (up to `WFBoomboxComponent.MaxQueued`, 10) with move up and remove. Tracks play out of the box for everyone
within earshot, following whoever carries it. A YouTube playlist or SoundCloud set link is listed without
downloading; entries that are private, deleted, live, members-only or over the length limit are skipped, and the
first 10 playable ones are queued. Starting from quiet waits out the request cooldown
(`wf.internet_sound.request_cooldown`); queueing is free. Examine shows what is playing, and the Stop music verb
silences it. Sold in the AutoTune vendor and by John Wolfgate, and a contractor loadout item; sprites are
/tg/station's boombox.

It obeys the same switches and limits as the PA (`wf.internet_sound.player_requests`, the concurrent track cap,
length and size limits), and every track lands in the admin logs. Admins see boombox tracks in the internet sound
window and can stop them with everything else.

Entry points: `WFBoomboxSystem` (queue, playlist reading, keys, examine, sprite) and the client `WFBoomboxWindow`.
The playback itself is generic in the Audio module: `InternetSoundSourceComponent`,
`InternetSoundSystem.PlayFromSource` and `ExpandPlaylist` on the server, and the client `InternetSoundSourceSystem`,
which plays the track from the item at its place on the timeline.
