# RMCClothing

Military clothing from [RMC-14](https://github.com/RMC-14/RMC-14) for the TSF and PDV: about 320 armors, uniforms,
helmets, hats, coats, ghillie suits and cloaks, neck items, scarves, boots, gloves, goggles, packs, satchels, chest
rigs and belts. TSF gets the UNMC marine gear, PDV the SPP army and colonial militia gear, and both get the generic
field gear. Ids start with `WFRMC`.

RMC draws marine gear in camouflage patterns picked by the map. Here a family drawn in camouflage is one item, and its
wearer picks the pattern in the round from the item's Pattern verbs (`WFCamoPatternSystem`), which copy the visuals
of a hidden `WFRMCLook...` prototype per pattern. Colour variants (ties, armbands, scarves) stay separate items.

Every item is a reskin: it takes the TSF or PDV item it replaces as its parent (armor takes the bulletproof vests'
stats, packs and belts their storage, snow suits a winter coat's warmth, goggles the faction HUD) and only swaps the
sprite, name and description. Players pick them in the faction loadout groups; the single-choice glove, glasses and
PDV jumpsuit groups are now visible. The Crocus infantry kits are left uniform. Marine armor is kept to one plate
style per cut; RMC's other plate styles only repeat the same silhouette.

TSF role gear goes to its role: every marine gets the M3, M3-L, M3-XL, M4, scout and flak armor; engineers the
M3-EOD and M3-T armor and technician and demolitions helmets; fireteam leaders the B12, grenadier, pyrotechnician,
ML66A and XM4 armor and helmets (captains get the B12 too); corpsmen the corpsman helmet and fatigues; captains and
colonels the officer armor and helmet, and colonels the commanding officer armor. The generator offers such an item
only in its roles' loadout groups, and where a group is shared with other roles (the marines' armor group), the
loadout carries `WFRoleWhitelistLoadoutEffect` so it shows as locked to everyone else.

The neck slot gets RMC's uniform accessories as neck items: ponchos, ties, armbands, armor paint, shoulder patches,
the M3 neck guard, shoulder pads, bracers and leg guards, and the general's formal cloak, with the accessory state
renamed to `equipped-NECK`. The drop, leg and armor pouches are small storage (`WFRMCNeckPouchBase`, 3x2, small
items). Scarves, neckerchiefs, smocks and torn scarves are offered in the neck slot too, as `WFRMCNeck...` twins
that share the mask item's sprite.

Ghillie suits and the M68 and V86 thermal cloaks (back slot) keep RMC's prepare position action
(`WFGhillieSuitSystem`): a four-second do-after, then the wearer fades to near invisible through RMCCore's stealth
until they move, are pulled, buckled or downed, or take the suit off. Each shot fired makes them a little more
visible.

The TSFMC field outfitter (Halcyon's marine ready room) and the Vanguard field outfitter (both walls of Helios's
spawn hall) sell every item of their faction in unlimited stock, for cash like the TSFMCDrobe; role-locked and
command gear stays in the loadouts.

Everything under the module's `Clothing` folders is generated, along with its blocks in the TSF and PDV loadout
groups. To change it, edit `Tools/_WF/RMCClothing/port.py` and run it from the repository root against an RMC-14
checkout:

```
python Tools/_WF/RMCClothing/port.py --source <RMC-14 checkout> --write
python Tools/_WF/Ci/modules.py --write
```
