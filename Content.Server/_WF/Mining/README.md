# Mining

Paces the material economy so the faction voucher ladder takes about 150 minutes to climb.

- Ores: gold, silver, plasma and uranium veins drop single ores, about a third of upstream. Lithium drops 0-2 per
  vein and is the main tuning knob. Sand and chromite rocks move from the 9 km ring to the Wilds; andesite leaves the
  ring.
- Scrap: the scrap processor smelts strategic sheets at 500 raw scrap (lithium 2,000) and diamond and bluespace at
  16,000, all without the upgrade discount. Lathe upgrade kits don't fit ore processors. Plastitanium is uncommon in
  scrap rolls and drone loot, and the crystallizer needs much more gas for plastitanium, plasteel and plasma.
- Research: the capital ships (Luda, Flyssa, Saturn) sit behind `WFTsfCapitalVouchers` and `WFPdvCapitalVouchers`,
  so each faction's top tier costs 585k research points. Research datafarm boards take 8 research disks.
- Hokkaido: the gas sale console pays MMC industrial credits, and the bounty board buys PDV composite
  (`WFMMCBountyPdvComposite`). Fissile uranium sells for 10,000 per sheet instead of 25,000.

The only code is `GasDepositSystem.Wolfgate.cs`. A gas sale console that pays another currency appraises in it
("N MIC"), and a sale that would pay nothing is refused, leaving the gas in the sale points. `MarketModifier`'s
`hideExamine` drops the rate line from examine, since the Hokkaido console states its rate in its description.
