# PlanetNpcGear

NPCs on a planet are dressed for its air, so the strangers, residents and squatters of a hot, frozen or unbreathable
world don't die where they spawn. Within a second of an NPC with an inventory turning up on a planet layer
(`WFPlanetLayer`), `WFPlanetNpcGearSystem` reads the world's air (its `MapAtmosphere`) against his body: a pressure
outside the warning band gets him an EVA suit, air within 20 K of his heat or cold damage threshold a fire suit or
winter gear, and air he can't breathe a gas mask and an emergency oxygen tank on internals in a free slot, which is
kept topped up for as long as he has it on him. The kits are startingGear in
`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/PlanetNpcGear/gear.yml`. NpcCrew bring their own EVA kit and are left alone, as are
players; mobs that don't breathe skip the mask.
