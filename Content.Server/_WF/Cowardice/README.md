# Cowardice

Civilians lose their nerve. A `WFCowardice` component on a stranger (beggar, preacher, conspiracist) or a killable
trader (wanderers, the planet mechanic and innkeeper, the minigame hosts) gives him two triggers: a player within
`gunRange` (8 tiles) who holds a gun and has it wielded, within 3 tiles or turned towards him (75 degrees either side
of the facing, in sight), and `hits` hits from a mob inside `hitWindow`. Each trigger rolls `cowardice`; a gunman is
only weighed once per 30 seconds. On a break he either cowers (`flee` is the share who run instead): down on the
ground, unable to move or attack, dropping what he holds with `dropChance` each, and crying out; or runs: a trader the
way he does when hurt (`TraderFleeSystem.Spook`, without refusing him custom later), anyone else 14 tiles away from the
threat by steering. After `minSeconds` to `maxSeconds` he calms down and cannot break again for `restSeconds`. A
stranger who broke stops fighting and drops the quarrel (`WFStrangerSystem.Frighten`), a trader ends the conversation
he was in. Combat NPCs (`WFCrew`) never carry the component; `CowardiceTest` checks it.

Per prototype: beggar 0.85 breaking at 2 hits, conspiracist 0.7 (runs 60%), preacher 0.5, innkeeper 0.4, other
traders and minigame hosts 0.6, the old miner 0.1. Drawn guns are noticed from players only (an `ActorComponent`).
