# Bonfires

Bonfires burn down. A bonfire (`Bonfire`, and so the campfires and camp fires built on it) holds seconds of fuel
(`WFBonfireFuel`): it starts a map with 30 minutes, burns one second a second while lit, and goes out when the fuel
is gone. Wood feeds it, by the wood in the item's material composition (3 seconds a unit, so a plank is 5 minutes
and a tower-cap log, at 200, is 10 minutes), and paper and books by tag; it holds at most an hour, and a full fire
takes no more. A lit flame (a lighter, a torch, a welder) lights a fire that has fuel; with none there is nothing to
burn. Examining it says whether it burns well, burns low, or is out and what it needs.

A fire that is out gives no warmth (`EntityRadiusHeaterSystem.IsActive` asks), so it neither warms bodies nor melts
snow, its light and crackle go off, it stops lighting things (`AlwaysHot`), and its sprite shows the burnt logs
(`WFBonfireVisuals.Lit`, drawn by a `GenericVisualizer`). `LegionnaireBonfire` (lava-world decor) and the cavern camp's
fire are `infinite` and never go out. Fireplaces are not fuelled.

Entry points: `WFBonfireFuelSystem.AddFuel`, `SetLit`, `FuelOf`.
