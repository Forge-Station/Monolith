# PlanetComms

Planetary comms ride a world's mass transceivers. The lore: Wolfgate's frame-dragging smears the phase of ordinary
radio below a world's orbit, so a mast that rides signals on a mass-modulated carrier is the only way on or off. A
planet network (every layer, caverns to orbit) is up while any `WFMassTransceiver` on it is powered and whole;
otherwise it is dark.

On a dark world headset, intrinsic and intercom radio can't send or hear (the speaker gets a rate-limited "no signal"
popup), announcements from off-world drop its players, and a global comms console on it refuses with "no uplink".
Exempt: the `WFCrew` crew channel, the `Handheld` band, handheld radios (a telecom-exempt item with its own mic or
speaker) and ghosts. Announcements whose source is on the same world, such as a ship PA or a station announcement from
a console there, still reach it, since they're wired. `wf.planet_comms false` turns the gate off.

Players keep a world up by powering its transceiver (3 kW off an APC). An EMP knocks it out until the pulse wears off;
300 damage wrecks it into `WFMassTransceiverBroken` through its construction graph (`WFMassTransceiverGraph`). A
wreck comes back working with 5 steel sheets, a screwdriver, then a welder. Every world gets one comms outpost
(`Resources/Maps/_WF/PlanetComms/comms_outpost.yml`, an entry in each PlanetPois table) at a random spot, a footpath from its gate to the roads, its
transceiver broken and its RTGs wired through a wall substation to the APC, so a world is dark until someone repairs
it and needs nothing else to come back up.

Entry points: `WFPlanetCommsSystem` (`IsCommsUp`, `CanReach`, `Reachable`/`TryNarrow` for announcement filters,
`Ungated` for out-of-character lines that ride the announcement path, and the radio send and receive attempt
handlers) and `ChatSystem.PlanetComms.cs`, which the marked announcement edits in `ChatSystem` call.
