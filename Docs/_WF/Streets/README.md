# Streets

Street furniture, road tiles and road decals for furnishing planet cities, ported from RMC-14 and Colonial Marines
Universe with their ids kept. It is content only: no C#. Mappers and admins place everything; nothing is craftable.

What is here, all under the `Streets` category of the spawn menu:

- Lights: colony streetlights, traffic lights, tall and small floodlights, each with a broken twin it turns into when
  destroyed. They are always on.
- Signs: road signs and billboards (free-standing and wall-mounted), neon shop signs, traffic directors, graffiti.
- Furniture: benches, a concrete planter and statue, a basketball hoop, phone boxes, dumpsters, trash bins, trash bags.
- Buildings and yards: rooftop and wall vents, colony fences, handrails, steel and plastic road barriers, raised
  platforms and their edges, catwalks and rails, cargo containers, barrels and tents.
- Tiles: asphalt and cement with their road markings, Hybrisa's asphalt, sidewalks, kerbs and dirt, and its shop and
  office floors (`RMCFloorAsphalt*`, `RMCFloorCement*`, `RMCPlanetHybrisa*`, `RMCFloorHybrisa*`).
- Decals: road edges and lines, stop marks, tyre marks, warning stripes, litter and grime (`RMCDecalHybrisa*`,
  `RMCDecalRoad*`).

Everything but `base.yml`, `Furniture/trash_bins.yml` and `Buildings/rails.yml` is generated by `Tools/_WF/Streets/port_streets.py` from
`Tools/_WF/Streets/port.yml`, which lists the source files and the rules: components Wolfgate lacks are dropped (xenos,
acid, the colony reactor, dropships, building recipes), sprites point at the copies under `Resources/Textures/_WF/Streets`,
and tiles and decals are flattened out of their parent chains. To bring over more, add the file to `port.yml` and run the
script; `--report` prints what it dropped and `--check` fails when the committed files are stale. The source checkouts sit
beside this one, as for the Vehicles tools.

RMC's trash bins are disposal units on its own pipe network, so `trash_bins.yml` rebuilds them as plain storage bins from
the same sprites. Its handrails and road barriers hang off its barricade base, so `rails.yml` rebuilds them as
railings on upstream's pattern, with RMC's ids. Not ported: the working payphone, landing-zone lights, landing signs named after RMC's colonies,
indoor furniture, flora and RMC's decorative stairs.

`assets.md` lists every texture with its source path, licence and copyright. RMC-14's prototypes are MIT and Colonial
Marines Universe's AGPL-3.0; the art is CC-BY-SA-3.0 unless its `meta.json` says otherwise.
