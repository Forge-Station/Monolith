# Leviathan

Great segmented worms, ported from Goob Station's space whale (its sprite and ambience; the tail system is reworked).

- **Chromite leviathan** (`WFAerumnaLeviathan`): Aerumna's local threat. One or two are let loose on the open ground
  each round (the `roamers` list on the world's POI table, spawned once the whole ground exists). It wanders far and
  hunts anything it sees, with a bite of about 16 brute and 500 health. It has no structural damage and collides like
  any mob, so walls, doors and hulls stop it.
- **Space whale** (`SpaceLeviathan`, `SpaceLeviathanDespawn`): Goob's original, 65 segments and hull-tearing bite, for
  admins to let loose in space. Nothing spawns it on its own.

`WFTailedEntityComponent` gives an entity a body of segments that follow the head like a chain.
