# PlanetNatives

Every NPC that comes into being on a planet (its ground, caverns or sky layers, not orbit) is a planet native
(`WFPlanetNativeComponent`), marked by `WFPlanetNativeSystem` as its brain map-initialises. Natives never pick each
other as targets, whatever their factions: wildlife, residents, strangers, camps, road patrols, site guardians, city
fates and turrets all leave each other alone and keep their usual feelings about players and visitors. The truce sits
in `NpcFactionSystem.GetNearbyHostiles` and also covers retaliation after a stray hit. A native with a mind (a player
in it) is no longer covered.
