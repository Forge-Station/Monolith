# Minigames

NPCs who play games for a stake. A host is a trader (`_WF/Traders`) with `WFMinigameHost`: a dialogue option with the
`PlayMinigame` action names one of its offers (game, difficulty, stake, optional item rewards) by index. The host asks
the customer to confirm the stake, takes it like any trader payment (cash on the counter, then the bank with their ID
there), and opens the game window. The server holds the game and checks every move; the client only draws it and sends
moves. A win pays the stake times the game's payout in cash on the counter, or the offer's items instead. A winner
waits `cooldown` (10 min) before that host plays them again, and a host stops taking games once a win could push its
payouts past `dailyCap` in 24 hours. Closing the window or walking off mid-game loses the stake.

Entry points: `MinigameSystem` and `WFMinigameHostComponent` (server), the rules engines and `MinigameRules` (every
number) in `Content.Shared/_WF/Minigames`, and the client `MinigameBoundUserInterface` / `MinigameWindow` with a panel
per game. Hosts are in `Resources/Prototypes/_WF/Minigames/hosts.yml`; riddles are in `riddles.ftl`.

Hosts: Vesna Kade (`WFMinigameDealer`, Higher or Lower 1000, Liar's Dice 2000, Lockbox 2500, all normal) and Old
Tamsin (`WFMinigameSage`, Riddles 500 and Memory 1000 normal, Ore Piles 5000 hard) at the Caelestinus Central bar
lounge; Dusty Rourke (`WFMinigameGambler`, easy Ore Piles 500, Liar's Dice 1000, Higher or Lower 500) at the roadside
inn's bar counter, beside the innkeeper; and the Old Wise Miner, Ezra Holloway (`WFMinigameOldMiner`), not placed: a cave encounter puts him down with
a table or crate in front. He pays in goods: easy Ore Piles (500) a stack each of gold and silver ore, normal Liar's
Dice (1500) two stacks of diamond ore and one of uranium, hard Ore Piles (3000) a diamond mining drill and five stacks
of diamond ore.

## The games and the house edge

Payout is a multiple of the stake (easy / normal / hard). "Returns" is what a win pays times how often the stated play
wins, per credit staked; below 1 the house keeps an edge. `MinigameRulesTest` computes or checks each.

| Game | Rules | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ore Piles | Misere Nim, take 1-3, last one loses, player first. The house deals a losing pile (13, 17, 21) 85 / 90 / 87.5 % of the time, else 14-20 without 17; the NPC plays perfectly but slips 6 / 4 / 0 % of moves | 3x | 4x | 6x |
| Liar's Dice | 5 dice each, no wilds, player opens; win 2 / 2 / 3 rounds in a row; NPC slips 3 / 0 / 0 % of turns | 3x | 3.5x | 6x |
| Lockbox | 4-digit code, digits 0-9 repeating, hits and near; 5 / 4 / 3 tries | 2.5x | 4x | 6x |
| Higher or Lower | Each card from a fresh deck, ties lose; ladder easy 1.2-1.5-1.9-2.4-3.0, normal 1.25-1.6-2-2.5-3.2-4, hard 1.3-1.7-2.2-2.9-3.8-4.8-6 | up to 3x | up to 4x | up to 6x |
| Memory | Repeat a growing sequence of 4 pads to 7 / 10 / 13; flash 0.6 / 0.45 / 0.3 s | 2x | 3x | 4x |
| Riddles | 2 / 3 / 4 riddles in a row from 24, four answers each | 2x | 3x | 5x |

Returns against the best play:

- Ore Piles, exact for a perfect player: wins 27.8 / 19.2 / 12.5 %, so 0.83 / 0.77 / 0.75 back. Hard never loses a
  losing deal (checked over every line of play).
- Higher or Lower, exact best stopping: 0.88 / 0.92 / 0.975 back.
- Lockbox normal and hard, proven: no strategy opens more than 1 + 13 + 13^2 (+ 13^3) of the 10,000 codes within 3 (4)
  tries, so at most 0.11 / 0.95 back. Easy is not proven: a strong solver (most-partitions guesses) opens 34.9 % of
  codes within 5 tries, so about 0.87 back; it opens 6.7 % within 4 and 1.0 % within 3.
- Liar's Dice is not solvable; a player bot that reasons like the NPC wins 27.5 / 26.9 / 13.9 % of 3000 seeded
  games, so 0.82 / 0.94 / 0.83 back. A sharper player may do better.
- Memory and Riddles are skill and knowledge games with no edge against a perfect player (notes, or the Fluent file).
  Only the cooldown and the daily cap limit them, so their payouts are the lowest.
