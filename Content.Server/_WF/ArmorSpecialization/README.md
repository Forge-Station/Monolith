# ArmorSpecialization

Worn-armour specializations that change what Wolfmed does to the body under a suit, so suits in a tier feel different
in a fight and not only on paper. Each is one component on the suit, and each respects the suit's armour coverage
(an item with no coverage list covers every part). The design and the faction assignments are in
`Docs/_WF/ArmorBalance/SPECIALIZATIONS.md`; how the code works, how to add a specialization and how to port it are in
`Docs/_WF/ArmorSpecialization/GUIDE.md`.

- `WFWoundWard`: refuses wounds outright (`blocks`), refuses single wound rules (`blockRules`, such as thrown
  dislocations for impact dampers) or makes a rule need a multiple of its usual damage (`hardens`). It answers
  Wolfmed's `WolfmedWoundRuleAttemptEvent`. The spall liner, sealed layer, insulation, tendon guards, concussion ward,
  bone plating, shock absorbers, trauma gel, cave-in rating, impact dampers, cold seal and comedic padding are all this
  component with different wound lists, and `examine` names which one it is.
- `WFLimbLock`: covered limbs stay on through finishing hits and blasts, unless one damage type of the hit reaches
  `breakthrough` (zero means never). It answers `WolfmedSeverAttemptEvent`; surgery doesn't raise it.
- `WFPainDampener`: adds `relief` (15 by default; an analgesic gives 22) to the wearer's pain relief, through
  `WolfmedArmorPainReliefEvent`.
- `WFAblativeCoat`: a passive pool that soaks a share of one damage group (Burn by default), or of one damage type
  (`damageType`, Heat for fire-rated suits), at the armour step, then recovers after a lull without hits.
- `WFAutoTourniquet` (server): ties a real Wolfmed tourniquet (`TourniquetSystem.Apply`) onto a covered limb with an
  untreated arterial bleed, spending a charge each time. Limbs only, so the necrosis clock and loosening work as normal;
  it never overrides a medic's treatment, works only while worn, and its charges come back over time.
- `WFPlasmaReserve` (server logic): 90u of universal synthetic plasma that feeds one patient over a line run with the
  "Transfuse from suit" verb on them, and never the wearer. The line pulls free past `range`; blood packs used on the
  suit refill it, one pack a use, sharing the IV drip's rule for which bodies take blood. Wolfmed's
  `WolfmedIvLineSystem` draws the line and shows the IV Connected alert. Medic suits carry it.
- Civilian suits also reuse upstream components: `NoSlip` for anti-slip treads, and an always-on `ItemToggle` with
  `MiningScanner` for ore sense.
- `WFCivilianSpecializationSystem` (shared) answers events as they happen:
  - `WFSteadyHands`: treatment breaks only past three times Wolfmed's interrupt line.
  - `WFSterileWeave`: covered wounds can't go bad, and wounds the wearer treats are cleaned.
  - `WFServoAssist`: tool work speeds up, through a marked hook in `SharedToolSystem`.
  - `WFRadSink`: takes half of incoming radiation.
  - `WFWildlifePadding`: cuts creature damage.
  - `WFHaulFrame`: undoes the pull slowdown.
  - `WFBlastLiner`: refuses severs, organ bruises and internal bleeds from explosions.
  - `WFVowOfSilence`: silences pain noises.
  - `WFSuitThrusters`: a zero-G burst action.
- The same shared system answers two marked hooks: the EMP system skips anything worn or carried by an EMP-hardened
  wearer (`WFEmpHardened`), and the reaction system skips touch reactions on a decon-coated wearer
  (`WFDeconCoating`). Showers and antiseptic spray go through `BypassDecon`.
- `WFSuitUpkeepSystem` (server) runs the passives that take time:
  - `WFStasisWeave`: holds every bleed through Wolfmed's stasis hold when the wearer goes critical.
  - Rad sink shedding.
  - `WFLivingTissue`: closes small bleeds.
  - `WFHonkOnHit`: the clown's honk.

`WFArmorSpecializationTest` covers each one through the real Wolfmed paths.
