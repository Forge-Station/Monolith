# NpcVehicleCrew

NPC-crewed ground vehicles for planets: a tank, APC, humvee or truck spawned with a driver, a gunner and troopers
aboard, driven and fought by the server. The crew are ordinary NpcCrew crewmen seated in the vehicle with their HTN
asleep; a 5 Hz brain steers the vehicle through the autopilot input, aims and fires the turret, and decides what the
vehicle does. Kill the driver and it stops; kill the gunner and the turret goes quiet. Crews are weaker than players
(slow first shot, aim error), only fight faction hostiles or whoever attacks them, never target player ships, and say
nothing: a guard vehicle warns with its horn.

Behaviours:

- Hold: parked at its post; the turret engages, and a guard crew honks at strangers inside `guardRadius`.
- Patrol: loops through waypoints round home; armed crews that can fire on the move keep rolling, a tank stops to fight.
  A road patrol (`patrolPauseMax` of 2 s or less) rolls through each waypoint on to the next without slowing for it,
  planning the next leg ahead; it stops only at a turnaround or the end of the line. Longer pauses stop at every point.
- Hunt: closes on a target to the prototype's standoff distance, then holds and shoots.
- Escort: follows a lead entity and defends it.
- Flee: unarmed vehicles drive away from whatever hit them.
- Retreat: automatic below `retreatIntegrity` or out of ammo; the vehicle drives home and still returns fire.
- Bail: automatic below `bailIntegrity` or when the vehicle can't run; the crew dismounts and guards its post on foot.

A crew is a `wfVehicleCrew` prototype (vehicle, seat roles, profile, faction, behaviour and tuning); the roster is in
`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/NpcVehicleCrew/crews.yml`. Admins spawn one with
`wf_vehiclecrew spawn <id> [behaviour] [distance]` (also `list` and `clear`), or place a `WFVehicleCrewSpawner*` marker
from the spawn menu's vehicle group, which spawns its crew when the map loads. Encounters and planet sites call
`WFVehicleCrewSystem.TrySpawn`; planet road patrols give it waypoints down the road (`SetWaypoints`), and a crew's
`patrolPauseMin`/`patrolPauseMax` set how long it stops at each.

The admin tab's **NPC vehicle crews** button opens a panel (`WFVehicleCrewPanel`, served by `WFVehicleCrewSystem.Panel`):
pick a crew from the roster, its orders, how many and how far away, and spawn them beside you; the live crews list
shows each one's orders, crew alive and hull, and lets you give new orders (Escort follows you), make where you stand
its home, go to it, delete it or clear them all. Each crew prototype's `name` is what the panel calls it.

Design notes are in `Docs/_WF/NpcVehicleCrew/design.md`.
