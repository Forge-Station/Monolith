# CavernEncounters

Encounters in the caverns (`_WF/Caverns`), spread so that exploring the tunnels turns one up every couple of minutes,
each a room cleared of rock in the cavern's own floor with its props and its people or creatures, and each giving
itself away before you see it.

- Placement (`WFCavernEncounterSystem`): when a world's network is built, each cavern draws its encounters from its
  `wfCavernEncounterTable` and a spot for each from its seed, in the ring between 40 tiles from the centre and 32 in
  from the edge, 45 tiles apart. The count follows the ring's area (`perArea`, 1.4 per 10,000 square tiles, clamped to
  `minCount`-`maxCount`): Asclepiu 54, Merak 36, Fervidus and Thrascias 21, Aerumna and Carcinoma 11 at their own
  radii. The first `nearGate` (2) spots go within 70 tiles past the centre's clearance, where the gate comes down.
  Kinds are picked by weight up to their `limits` (the miner camp, the Echo and the Lamplighter one each, surveyors
  and trapped miners four each). Nothing spawns until a player comes within 48 tiles on the cavern; then the spot, or the nearest of 24 shifts of it up to 30 tiles away, is checked (`Fits`): clear of
  every mouth, cut stairs and slime grotto, nothing pinned under it on either map (sites, cellars, stairs, mouth
  pads, other rooms), nothing built in it, and open tunnel just past its edge. The room's tiles are laid from the
  cavern's noise, what the biome grew there removed and the tiles pinned, and the layout spawned
  (`wfCavernEncounter`: entries of prototypes, counts and distances from the centre). An encounter with no room is
  skipped for good. `SpawnEncounter` lays one out anywhere, unchecked, for tests and admins.
- The Old Wise Miner's camp (fire, bedroll, rack, picks, ore sacks, lantern). His NPC and games come from the
  Minigames module: the camp's entry is `pending: WFMobOldWiseMiner`, spawned once that prototype exists.
- Tunnel raiders (`WFCavernRaiderSystem`): 3-5 with shivs and pipe shotguns round a fire. Each runs from whoever hurt
  him once he has taken 45 damage (counted before the wound system shares it out), and drops scrap and 20-120 spesos
  when he dies.
- The lost surveyor (`WFCavernSurveyorSystem`): spawns hurt; activate him and he follows you, comes along when you
  climb or drop a level within 4 tiles of him, and pays you 1,500 once he stands alive on the ground above.
- The trapped miner (`WFTrappedMinerSystem`): walled in by a ring of the cavern's own rock; dig out any rock of it and
  he thanks you, leaves 800 spesos and walks off.
- Blind crawlers (`WFCavernCrawlerSystem`): a nest that ignores whoever walks and goes for whoever runs within 10 tiles
  (faster than halfway between their walk and run speed), dropping them after 4 s without running.
- The Lamplighter (`WFLamplighterSystem`): can't move or strike while a switched-on handheld light shines on it (in
  the light's radius, unoccluded), and puts out the nearest such light within 7 tiles every 9 s.
- The Echo (`WFCavernEchoSystem`): learns what humanoids say within 12 tiles and says it back in their names while
  someone is within 24 tiles but none within 3; whoever comes within 3 is attacked.
- An abandoned camp: a lantern still burning, a bedroll and a little gear and scrap. Filler that pays a little.
- Telegraphs (`WFCavernTelegraphSystem`): a `WFCavernTelegraph` plays its sound now and then, carrying 20 tiles, while
  a player is within 20 tiles (a dead mob stays quiet): the camps' fire (`WFCavernCampfire`, which also throws an
  8-tile glow) crackles, raiders laugh, cough and key their radios, crawlers click and the Echo whispers. The
  Lamplighter's lair is ringed with lit omen lanterns (`WFCavernOmenLight`) that die with a fizzle, one by one, as a
  player comes within 14 tiles of each; the Lamplighter neither douses them nor freezes in their light.
- Admins: `cavernencounters [world]` lists every world's encounters, numbered, with state, planned spot and room
  centre; `cavernencounters goto <n>` lays the numbered one out if nobody has been near and moves the caller to its
  room's edge.

Skipped from the owner's list: the hermit ore buyer (the Traders module has no service that buys from players),
the rogue drill rig, spore sleepers and the burrowing worm (each a new behaviour too many for one lane).

Sprites are the repo's own, recoloured in the prototypes; sounds are the owner's Black Mesa set, imported by
`Tools/_WF/LivingWorld/import_bms.py --encounters` into `Resources/Audio/_WF/CavernEncounters` (`CavernEncounterTest`
fails on an unused file or more than 16), plus the repo's own laughs, coughs, radio clicks and whispers.
