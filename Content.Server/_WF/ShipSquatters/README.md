# ShipSquatters

A bit of fun for landed ships nobody is minding. A player's ship (it holds a deed; never a station, an NPC crew's ship, an
encounter's wreck or a hull in flight) sitting on a planet's ground with no living player, sleeper with a mind or NPC
crewman aboard or within 32 tiles for 20 minutes may get a squatter. Every minute an idle ship rolls 2%, so an hour's walk
away is about a coin flip and a five-minute errand never counts. One squatter a ship for good, and at most three alive per
world. All in cvars: `wf.ship_squatters`, `_idle_minutes`, `_roll_seconds`, `_chance`, `_cap`, `_guard_range`.

He appears on a clear tile with air indoors, as far from the airlocks as the ship allows, with a bedsheet and a duffel for a
bedroll, in clothes for the world's air (`PlanetNpcGear`) and a spear. Nothing is opened, broken or moved to place him.

- **Sitting:** he wanders the rooms joined to his bed (no wall or shut door between, so he opens nothing), lingers and
  insists the ship is his: barks when someone boards or comes close and when talked at, and a Talk verb opens a dialogue
  (the strangers' window) where asking, pleading, offering money and threatening are all turned down. Cash handed to him is
  refused. He never flees, attacks, breaks, moves, steals, flies the ship or pays anyone.
- **Mess:** every 45 to 120 seconds, up to 24 a ship, he leaves litter (existing trash items), vomit or beer puddles or a
  graffiti decal on bare floor in his rooms: no tile with anything anchored on it, none beside a door, none on a puddle. It
  all mops or picks up. It stops when he is dead, off the hull or angry.
- **Forced:** a blow, a shot, being pulled or dragged, cuffed or shoved turns him on whoever did it (not on their friends:
  there is no party concept to hook). His own brain takes over, and `WFNpcIntent` (`WFIntentsSquatter`) has him stop on a
  downed target and leave them be for 5 minutes unless they hit him again. A forcer who is down, gone 35 tiles or dead is
  dropped; with none left he glares for 3 minutes, then calms down and goes back to insisting. Carried off the hull, he walks
  back if he can. If the ship takes off he goes with it, mess and all.

Entry points: `WFShipSquatterSystem` (`.Spawn.cs` idle watch and placement, `.Mess.cs`, `.Talk.cs`).
