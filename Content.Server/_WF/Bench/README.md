# Bench

The Common Writ, the sector's in-character law, and the tools Caelestinus judges use to enforce it. The laws are
`wfLaw` prototypes grouped by `wfLawChapter` (`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/Bench/laws.yml`); the Common Writ page
(`WFCommonWrit`, under Caelestinus in the lore book) and the app both read them. Judges spawn with `WFBookCommonWrit`,
a book that opens that page. Items listed in the Writ's Schedule of Unlawful Material carry `Contraband` with the
`WFUnlawful` severity, so examine marks them. `Docs/_WF/Bench/LAWBOOK.md` is the review copy.

**Judges.** A judge (`SecurityGuard`, `WFBenchSystem.JudgeJobs`) spawns with the Bench app on their PDA. A
`WFBenchTerminal` gives the same tools in place. Ticket tab: pick someone within `wf.bench.range` (3 tiles), tick laws
and set each fine inside its range, add posted ordinances or custom charges, then Issue. Ledger tab: the round's
tickets, and Pardon. Laws tab: the Writ, plus ordinances judges post for the round. Only judges can act; anyone else
holding the app only reads the laws.

**Fines.** `WFBenchSystem.Issue` takes what the offender's bank holds into the NFSD sector account, hands the judge a
stamped `WFBenchTicket` paper and writes a line to their sector criminal record. The rest is Bench debt for the round:
`WFBenchSystem.Fines.cs` watches `BalanceChangedEvent` and takes `wf.bench.garnish` (half) of each later gain. Debt
past `wf.bench.deadline_minutes` (30) or over `wf.bench.debt_cap` (50,000) sets the record Wanted until it is paid.
An Outlawry charge carries no fine: Wanted at once and a `WFShipbreakerWarrant` bounty (`wf.bench.outlaw_bounty`).
Soft limits stop a ticket being used to grief: each law or ordinance counts once per ticket, at most
`WFBenchMath.MaxCharges` (8) charges, custom fines up to `MaxCustomFine` (100,000), and `WFBenchMath.Cap` holds a
ticket to `wf.bench.ticket_cap` (200,000) and a person to `wf.bench.round_cap` (400,000) a round, pardons excluded.
A pardon refunds everything taken, drops the debt and lifts what the ticket caused. Everything resets at round end.

`WFBenchMath` (Shared) holds the sums and is unit-tested; `WFBenchTest` covers the bank, garnish, overdue, outlawry and
pardon flow. Impound and banishment are recorded on the ticket and enforced by judges in character.

**Judge ship refits** touch hardpoints only. The Roswell's port bow hardpoint carries `WFBenchHarpoonGun`, an ARACHNE
hacking harpoon whose `WFBenchHarpoon` draws a fresh dart two minutes after each shot (`WFBenchHarpoonSystem`), in
place of a Cerberus. The Lightning swaps its starboard outer TOVEK for a SHRIKE ion disruptor (`WFIonDisruptor`) to
stop ships that won't heave to.
